## When to use
When the user asks to fill/record Payment Details for new **Bupa, Tawuniya, MedGulf or Malath** deposits in a BANK workbook (sheet like "BANK 2025-2026"), matching them against the insurer's statement file OR against this workbook's own "2026/2025 SOA BY TPA" sheets. Also use when the user asks to check whether confirmed deposits were posted into the statement of account ("كشف الحساب" / "مدفوعات كشف الحساب" / Collection). This is the only skill for these four insurers — older versions (…-matching, -v2..-v7) are superseded.

## v8 corrections (read first — these caused real errors in production)

**1. Month and batch/SOA-row matching are TWO SEPARATE DECISIONS. Never leave Payment Details (AG) blank just because the SOA batch/row can't be pinned down — AND once the month is known, distribute into the SPECIFIC MONTH column, not the year column.**
Payment Details records the CLAIM MONTH. The claim month is usually inferable independently of which SOA batch (Bupa) or which specific row will eventually consume it — from the deposit date and the insurer's known lag (Bupa ~1 month, MedGulf ~2 months, Malath 1-3 months, Tawuniya ~1 month), and, most reliably, from the **lag pattern of sibling deposits**: if 6 other deposits dated the same week/month for the same insurer all turned out to be paying month M, an unmatched 7th deposit from the same batch of wires is almost certainly also paying month M, even if you can't tell which specific SOA batch/branch-row it belongs to.
- If the month is confidently inferable: **write AG = that month's serial immediately**, and **distribute the amount into that specific month's column** (I–AF in "BANK 2025-2026" — read the frozen header row fresh and find the column whose serial equals the claim month; never guess by column letter). Do this even if the SOA collection posting (which row/batch gets credit) stays open.
- **Only fall back to a YEAR column (A–H) when the sheet genuinely has no monthly breakdown** (e.g. a "By Year" mirror sheet with just 8 year columns) — check the header row first. If both a month column AND a year column exist on the same sheet and the month is known, the month column is correct and the year column must stay empty; do not fill both, and do not default to the year column "to be safe" when the month is already confirmed. A prior version of this skill made exactly this mistake — it knew the month (Jul-2026) but still wrote the amount into the YEAR-2026 column instead of the Jul-2026 monthly column, on the theory that "distributing by year alone is enough for DIFFRANCE=0." DIFFRANCE nets to 0 either way, but that is NOT sufficient proof of correctness — the aging/monthly breakdown is wrong until the amount sits in the actual month column. The user caught this twice in one session.
- Only the SOA-side posting (which collection cell gets the formula) should stay open/flagged when the batch is genuinely unidentifiable. Note on the BANK cell should say "month confirmed and distributed to its month column, SOA batch/row still open" — do not conflate the month decision with the batch decision, and do not describe a year-column fallback as done when a month column was available.

**2. Blank-detection on Payment Details (AG) MUST be trim-based, never `=== ""`.**
A cell can contain a single-space or whitespace-only string (`" "`) which looks blank on screen but is NOT `===""` and NOT `typeof x !== "string"`. Any script that scans for "available/unassigned deposits" by checking `ag === "" || ag === null` will silently skip these cells — they never enter the matching pool, and the underlying real deposit gets falsely reported as "no matching deposit found," causing a valid SOA collection to be wrongly left blank/red-flagged.
- Always check: `typeof ag !== "number" && (ag||"").toString().trim() === ""` for "is this Payment Details cell actually empty."
- After ANY bulk "leave blank, no candidate found" conclusion, re-scan the full candidate range specifically for whitespace-only AG cells before finalizing — this is a cheap, mandatory sanity check that catches this exact bug.

## Covered insurers (quick reference)
| Insurer | Streams | Lag | Exact bands | SOA batches? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bupa** | one (claims incl. VAT) | ~1 month (sometimes 2) | 61.75% or 67.2%; Doha Medical Clinics = 72.75% | **YES** (Insurance TBA col: BUPA - Regular / Regular2 / SABIC / ARAMCO / Saudi Arabian Airlines / Regular In Patient) |
| **Tawuniya** | claims + VAT paid separately | ~1 month | claims = 66.5% × net; VAT = 70% × VAT | No (single row / branch+month) |

| **Malath** | one | 1–3 months | **75.5%–77% band** (all branches); Dar Ram = 70% | No |

## Golden rule (from the user)
**If a deposit's ratio is far from the agreed band, do NOT record anything: highlight the cell red (#FFC7CE) and leave it empty for the user to review personally.** Recording a wrong month/amount is worse than leaving blank. The user's own direct confirmation of a month is the highest-authority signal — it overrides your computed tolerance check (record it with a yellow flag + a variance note).

**Important scope of this rule:** it applies to matching a deposit to a SPECIFIC CLAIM MONTH/SOA ROW when the ratio doesn't fit. It does NOT license leaving Payment Details or month-distribution blank/generic when the month is independently confirmable by other means (sibling lag pattern, deposit date, AK year field) — see v8 correction #1 above. Also — if a SOA sheet's own note documents that a prior human reviewer deliberately decided NOT to re-match a specific off-band row, treat that as a standing decision and do not silently override it; if you re-open it, say so explicitly and explain why.

## Sheet layout (confirm at execution — varies by workbook)
**BANK sheet** (e.g. "BANK 2025-2026"): cols A–H = YEAR-2019…YEAR-2026 Adj; I–AF = 24 monthly columns spanning two years, each header a date serial in the frozen row (e.g. I=45658 Jan-2025 … AF=46357 Dec-2026 — Jun appears twice, one per year, 12 columns apart); AG = Payment Details (date serial numFmt mmm-yy for a claims match, or text for a Tawuniya VAT entry); AH = Amount; AI = Date; AJ = Month; AK = Year; AL = INS TPA; AM = BRANCH; AN = Provider No.; AO = INS Company; AP = CHI; AQ = Bank; AR = DIFFRANCE (=AH − SUM(A:AF)). **This sheet HAS monthly columns — always distribute into the specific month column (I–AF) when the month is known, never into A–H.**

There is often a **mirror sheet** ("BANK 2025-2026 By Year") with the same records but a compressed **8-year-column-only layout** (A–H = YEAR-2019...YEAR-2026, no monthly columns at all, no "Adj" suffix) — Payment Details/Amount/etc. sit at a different column offset (e.g. I/J instead of AG/AH). **This sheet has NO monthly breakdown — the year column (A–H) is the correct and only distribution level here; do not treat this as inconsistent with the main sheet.** Match records between the two sheets by CHI + Amount (+ branch), and mirror every Payment Details write into BOTH sheets — mirror the amount distribution using whichever granularity each sheet actually supports (month column on the main sheet, year column on the By Year mirror). Verify DIFFRANCE = 0 in both, independently — check the header row of each sheet before deciding where to write, don't assume both sheets share the same column layout.

**SOA sheet** ("2026 SOA BY TPA" / "2025 SOA BY TPA" — pick by the YEAR of the claim month, not the deposit date): A Provider No.; **B "Insurance TBA" = the batch/sub-entity (matters for Bupa)**; C Insurance Co.; D Branch; E Month (serial); F Net; G VAT; H Total (=F+G); I Deductible; J Net+Deduct; K Vol.Disc; L P.P Disc; **M Collection (the Step-7 target)**; N Collection% (=M/H); **O "Expt P.P Claims TAW" = Net×66.5%** & **P "Expt VAT TAW P.P" = VAT×70%** (Tawuniya only — precomputed formulas); **Q "Expt P.P" = H × the branch's fixed band% (precomputed, e.g. =H×61.75%)** — Q is your most reliable per-batch expected-collection target for Bupa; R Expt%; S Rejection; T Rejection%; U O/S.

## Step 1 — Scope the empty Payment Details rows
Scan Payment Details for rows where INS TPA matches the requested insurer and Payment Details is empty — **use the trim-based blank check from v8 correction #2, never `=== ""`.** The user re-sorts/hides rows between and during sessions — never reuse row numbers from an earlier scan; re-locate and re-verify branch + amount before every write. Group same-branch/same-date deposits (installments, OR — for Bupa — separate sub-batch payments arriving together).
**Done when:** fresh list of empty rows with branch, amount, deposit date, confirmed via trim-based blank check.

## Step 2 — Get claim totals
Use the workbook's "2026/2025 SOA BY TPA" sheets directly when present — preserving the **B (batch) column for Bupa**. Build your lookup keyed by (insurer, branch, **batch**, month) for Bupa; (insurer, branch, month) for the other three.
**Done when:** data captured with exact branch (and Bupa batch) names, no sub-batch collapsed.

## Step 3 — Matching rules (band check to DECIDE the month)
Tolerance for "exact" = ±1 SAR unless the band is a range (Malath). Build the set of already-assigned months per branch (and Bupa batch) so you only match unpaid months.
- **Bupa**: deposit in month M pays claims of M-1 (occasionally M-2); sum ÷ total lands on 61.75% / 67.2% (Doha 72.75%). Test **every** sub-batch before concluding no fit — a large deposit usually belongs to a bigger sub-batch (Regular) not a small one (SABIC). If a sub-batch's row for the month doesn't exist yet in the SOA sheet, say so. If a deposit's month is confirmable via sibling lag pattern but no batch fits, record the month anyway (v8 correction #1) and leave only the batch/SOA-row question open.
- **Tawuniya**: claim = 66.5% × net (date serial); VAT = 70% × VAT (paid separately). Small branches may combine two months in one deposit — test 2-month sums.
- **MedGulf**: month M paid early-mid M+2; band 67/70/72%; SCECO 87.30% (may arrive net of a recon deduction — red-flag/note). A recent month with no deposit yet is simply **not due**, not a gap.
- **Malath**: lag 1-3 months. **Accepted band is the range 75.5%–77% of Total for all branches** (Dar Ram fixed 70%). A ratio inside the window counts as exact — do not require exactly 77%.

## Step 4 — Write the Payment Details match (three tiers)
1. **Exact / in-band / user-confirmed** → write month (or the VAT text) + a note with the math.
2. **Near miss (tens of SAR, unambiguous, not user-confirmed)** → write month + yellow #FFF2CC + note.
3. **Far from band / no plausible month / conflicting duplicates** → leave blank, red #FFC7CE + note "LEFT BLANK FOR REVIEW" naming every batch checked and whether the SOA row for the month exists yet.
4. **Month confirmable but batch/SOA-row not** (v8) → write the month (not blank!), distribute into its specific month column, and flag only the batch/posting question in the note — don't red-flag the month field itself.
**Done when:** every scoped row is filled-with-note or red-flagged-blank, and month vs. batch decisions are not conflated.

## Step 5 — Distribute the confirmed amount into its BANK monthly column (mandatory)
For each row filled in Step 4 (tiers 1–2 and the month part of tier 4), **check the sheet's header row first to see whether it has monthly columns (I–AF style) or only year columns (A–H style, e.g. a "By Year" mirror)**. If monthly columns exist, read the frozen header row **fresh** and find the column whose serial equals DATE(claim_year, claim_month, 1) — match on **full date (year AND month)**, never month-name or column-letter memory (Jun-2025 and Jun-2026 are 12 columns apart) — and write `=<Amount_cell>` there. Only write to a year column (A–H) if the sheet has no monthly columns, or if the month genuinely isn't known yet (only the year is). DIFFRANCE=0 proves *some* column got the amount, NOT that it is the right granularity or the right column — verify the serial before calling it done.
**Done when:** every tier-1/2/4 row has DIFFRANCE=0 AND the amount sits under the correct column at the finest granularity the sheet supports (month if available, year only if not), verified by read-back, in BOTH the main sheet and any "By Year" mirror (checked independently per sheet's own column layout).

## Step 6 — Clean up stray formatting
Rows moving from a prior red/yellow flag into a confirmed tier must have their fill explicitly reset to the sheet's normal background. Re-read values + notes + fills. If only the month was confirmed but the batch/posting is still open (v8 tier 4), clear the flag on the Payment Details/Amount cells (they're resolved) but keep any SOA-side flag open with an updated note explaining exactly what remains unresolved — and make sure the note names the actual column used (e.g. "distributed to Jul-2026 column AA123"), not a generic "distributed to year" if a month column was actually used.

## Step 7 — Post the confirmed collection into the SOA "Collection" column (MANDATORY)
A confirmed BANK match is **not finished** until its amount is recorded in the statement of account ("رحّل المبلغ في كشف الحساب"). Logic mirrors skill **bank-collection-posting-v4**:

- **Pick the SOA sheet by the YEAR of the claim month** ("2026 SOA BY TPA" vs "2025 SOA BY TPA"), not the deposit date.
- **Only post where SOA column M (Collection) is currently blank.** If all batches for a (branch, month) already have M filled, skip.
- **Write M as a live formula, not a static number** (see per-insurer forms below). Add an Excel comment where possible: source bank row(s)/amount, match type, resulting M/H% (off-band = a *warning*, not a reason to refuse — the month was decided in Steps 3–4).
- **Comment API caveat (observed):** cells may carry either a legacy Note or a modern threaded Comment, and they are different APIs — `workbook.comments.getItemByCell` throws "doesn't exist" on a legacy Note, and the set_cell_range `note` field throws "ItemAlreadyExists" on a cell that already has a Comment. Check which one exists (try comments API first; if it throws "doesn't exist", it's a legacy note — use set_cell_range's `note` field, which will overwrite it) before choosing how to update. When updating an existing annotation, **verify the row/column references you cite in the new note text actually match the current formula** — don't paste stale references from an earlier draft; re-extract them from the live formula right before writing the note.
- **Never let a `range.clear()` call target the wrong ClearApplyTo enum.** There is no `Excel.ClearApplyTo.notes` in this API surface — a conditional fallback like `X ? Excel.ClearApplyTo.notes : Excel.ClearApplyTo.contents` will silently clear CONTENTS (wiping the formula) when the "notes" member doesn't exist. If you need to clear only a note, do it via the note-specific write path (set_cell_range with an empty/replacement note, or the Comments API), never via `range.clear()`.
- Comments/notes are best-effort audit only: the M formula is self-documenting (it names its source BANK rows) and column N shows the ratio, so posting is fully traceable even when annotations don't attach cleanly. Do not let comment/note failures block or undo the value postings.

**BUPA (batch company) — match each deposit to its exact batch, NEVER aggregate:**
- For each (branch, month) group, list the blank batches (each with Q = H×band%) and the numeric-AG BANK deposits for that branch+month. Greedily assign each blank batch its single closest deposit where |deposit − Q| ≤ 1 SAR OR ≤ ~1–1.5% (each deposit used once). Post `M = ='BANK 2025-2026'!AH<bankRow>`.
- If a group has more blank batches than deposits, some batches simply **haven't been paid yet** — post the matches, leave the rest blank (note "deposit not arrived"). Never write one deposit that covers several batches into a single batch cell (anti-dump) UNLESS the combined deposit's amount matches the SUM of two (or more) batches' Q values to within a few cents — in that case it's safe to post each batch as a proportional-split formula referencing the same underlying deposit (`=ROUND('BANK...'!AH<row>*Q_this/(Q1+Q2),2)`), since the near-exact sum match proves the intended split. If the sum doesn't match closely, leave the group for manual review.
- Some existing placeholder cells (formulas like `=ROUND(Q<row>,2)` or `=ROUND(H<row>*<band>,2)` with NO real BANK reference) are not genuine postings even though they show a plausible-looking number — they are unbacked estimates. Treat them the same as blank/wrong when scanning for faults: check whether the formula actually references a real `'BANK...'!AH<n>` cell, not just whether it evaluates near the expected value.
- **Before overriding any existing formula-based M cell, check for a pre-existing Excel comment/note first.** If it documents a prior human reviewer's deliberate decision not to match an off-band deposit, do not silently overwrite it — see Golden Rule scope note above.

**TAWUNIYA — proven auto-post method:**
In BANK, Tawuniya is TWO rows per branch+month — a **claims** row (numeric AG = month serial, amount ≈ column O = Net×66.5%) and a **VAT** row (text AG). The SOA Collection convention (confirmed by the user, and by observation that M = O+P to the cent on clean rows) is **M = claims payment + VAT payment for that month**. Post it like this:
1. For each blank-M Tawuniya SOA row, find the **claim deposit**: numeric-AG BANK row, branch match, month == E serial, with |amount − O| ≤ max(1, O×0.6%).
2. Find the **VAT deposit**: any text-AG Tawuniya BANK row for the same branch with |amount − P| ≤ max(2, P×2%), each VAT row used only once, nearest-match tiebreak if multiple candidates. Matching on ≈P automatically picks the clean single-month VAT payment and ignores messy multi-month reconciliation blobs ("VAT Recon 11,12-2025", etc.) because a combined blob won't equal one month's P.
3. If **both** found → post `M = ='BANK 2025-2026'!AH<claimRow> + 'BANK 2025-2026'!AH<vatRow>`. Sanity check: resulting M/H should land in a consistent band for that month (bands can shift month to month, e.g. 57.8% one month, 66.9% another, 67.5% another — check consistency against sibling branches in the SAME month, not a single fixed global number).
4. If only the claim is found (its VAT is still inside a multi-month recon blob) or the claim hasn't arrived → leave M blank and log it; do NOT post claim-only, because the convention needs both. Small branches occasionally combine two months in one claim deposit — test 2-month sums before giving up.

**MEDGULF / MALATH (single row per branch+month) — direct post:**
- Branch+month uniquely identifies the SOA row, so once the month is confirmed in BANK, post `M = ='BANK 2025-2026'!AH<row>` (sum multiple confirmed deposits for the same branch+month) **directly, regardless of band** — an off-band ratio is a note/warning, never a reason to leave M blank, UNLESS a pre-existing comment/note shows a human already reviewed this exact row and deliberately chose not to post it (see Golden Rule scope note).
- MedGulf SCECO can arrive net of a recon deduction and is large-value — if its ratio is oddly off, leave for review and note it.

**Log every gap.** For anything not posted, record it (a dedicated "نواقص الترحيل" sheet works well) with: company, SOA sheet+row, batch, branch, month, Total(H), expected, and a status — **GAP** (deposit exists, postable/needs review) vs **PENDING** (no deposit yet / not due) vs **MONTH-ONLY** (v8: month confirmed and distributed to its month column, batch/SOA-row still open).
**Done when:** every confirmed deposit is posted to SOA M (formula, stale fill cleared), and every unposted blank is logged with a reason. Never claim "all posted" without naming the exceptions.

## Step 8 — Anti-dump check (Bupa) + verify + report
After Bupa posting, scan every touched (branch, month) group: if one batch holds a value ≈ the sum of all batches' expected while its siblings stay blank, fix by splitting. Then re-read all written cells (values + formulas + N ratio, checking for #REF!/#VALUE!) and report: counts posted per company, every yellow/red/blank exception by row + branch + amount + reason, and confirmation that DIFFRANCE=0 (BANK, and any By Year mirror, each per its own column layout) AND M is posted (SOA) for every confirmed row.
**Done when:** the read-back matches what you claim, with all exceptions named — and every "distributed" claim names the actual column (month or year) used.
